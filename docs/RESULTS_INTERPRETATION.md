# Results interpretation

The pilot changed **only the reader-facing rendering** of a fixed canonical contract. Findings:
- Reader-facing serialization causally altered executable contract use on fresh tasks.
- The dominant lever is **literal vs paraphrased predicate realization** (cache 13/16 literal vs 0/16
  paraphrase); action position was **not** implicated; control-plane/ID removal were secondary.
- The governed compiler's **refusal** of invalid contracts prevented old-fix adoption (safety win).

**Product verdict:** architecture **supported as a design direction**, but the preregistered acceptance
suite was **not fully passed** (3/5 gates). **Research verdict:** strong domain-scoped evidence for
interface-aligned literal memory rendering; no general coding-memory efficacy claim.
